Our tech hero

Safiya Umoja Noble

A professor and black feminist

Who They Are

Safiya Umoja Noble is a professor at the University of California, Los Angeles, and co-founder and faculty director of the UCLA Center on Race & Digital Justice.

How They Challenge Norms

She is the author of Algorithms of Oppression: How Search Engines Reinforce Racism (2018), which grew out of her discovery that a Google search for "Black girls" returned sexualized and pornographic content. Drawing on Black feminist thought, the book argues that search algorithms are not neutral and instead reproduce racist and sexist ideas, a pattern she describes as technological redlining. Her work extends to newer technologies, including AI systems, and calls on tech leaders and developers to take responsibility for the harms their products cause.She is a 2021 MacArthur Fellow and the recipient of the inaugural NAACP-Archwell Digital Civil Rights Award, which honors leaders advancing technology equity and social justice.

Why We Chose Them

Safiya Umoja Noble let as question the fairness of search engines and draw our attention to these tiny and easily overlooked biases in our daily use of technology
